# CIVIC ENGAGEMENT AND DEMOCRATIC GOVERNANCE PROGRAMME

## 1\. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW

The **Civic Engagement and Democratic Governance Programme** is a **10-hour, five-module learning programme** designed to equip students with the values, knowledge, practical skills and attitudes needed to become responsible, active and resilient citizens.

The programme connects **Zambia's National Values and Principles (NVP)** with practical learning on citizenship, community service, environmental responsibility, personal well-being and care for others. It uses participatory approaches that encourage students to understand their rights and responsibilities, engage constructively with their communities and contribute to positive social change.

The programme is aligned with relevant **UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** and **AU Agenda 2063**, with particular emphasis on democratic participation, good governance, sustainable communities, health and well-being, equality and the meaningful engagement of students in community development.

# 2\. THEORY OF CHANGE

### If

students are provided with practical knowledge of **national values, democratic governance, citizenship, community service, environmental responsibility, mental health and peer support**;

### and if

learning is delivered through **participatory discussions, practical activities, reflection, role-play, community-based exercises and guided action planning**;

### and if

students are connected to **appropriate school, community and professional support structures**;

### then

students will develop stronger **civic knowledge, ethical decision-making, confidence, resilience, collaboration and problem-solving skills**;

### leading to

greater **student participation in constructive community activities, responsible citizenship, respect for democratic principles, environmental stewardship and positive support for the well-being of others**;

### contributing to

**more engaged, responsible and resilient citizens who contribute to democratic governance, sustainable communities and Zambia's national development.**

### Theory of Change Pathway

**INPUTS**  
Facilitators + learning materials + school/community partnerships + safeguarding systems

↓

**ACTIVITIES**  
Five participatory learning modules + practical exercises + reflection + action planning

↓

**OUTPUTS**  
Students trained + community-service plans developed + environmental actions undertaken + support/referral pathways understood

↓

**SHORT-TERM OUTCOMES**  
Improved civic knowledge + stronger values + improved decision-making + increased awareness of well-being and support

↓

**MEDIUM-TERM OUTCOMES**  
Greater student participation + responsible behaviour + community engagement + environmental responsibility + appropriate help-seeking

↓

**LONG-TERM IMPACT**  
**More informed, responsible, engaged and resilient citizens contributing to democratic governance and sustainable community development.**

# 5\. SAFEGUARDING GUIDANCE

Safeguarding applies throughout the programme, with **additional safeguards required for Modules 4 and 5**.

The programme provides education and awareness; it is **not a substitute for counselling, psychotherapy, medical treatment or crisis intervention**.

### Core Safeguarding Principles

Facilitators should:

* Create a respectful, safe and non-judgmental learning environment.
* Explain that students do not have to disclose personal experiences.
* Avoid forcing students to discuss traumatic or private experiences.
* Use age-appropriate language.
* Know the school's safeguarding and referral procedures before sensitive sessions.
* Identify appropriate trusted adults and professional support services.
* Respond appropriately to disclosures of serious distress, abuse or immediate safety concerns.
* Never promise to keep serious safety concerns secret.

### Modules 4 and 5

For **Mental Health Resilience** and **Suicide Prevention**:

* Do not diagnose students.
* Do not conduct group counselling.
* Do not use graphic descriptions or suicide methods.
* Do not require students to disclose personal suicidal thoughts publicly.
* Role-play should focus on **recognising concerns, listening and connecting someone to help**.
* Students should understand that they are **not responsible for managing another person's crisis alone**.
* Immediate safety concerns should be referred through established safeguarding and professional pathways.

### Core Safeguarding Message

> \*\*CREATE SAFETY → LISTEN WITHOUT JUDGEMENT → DO NOT PROMISE SECRECY → CONNECT TO APPROPRIATE HELP → FOLLOW SAFEGUARDING PROCEDURES\*\*
